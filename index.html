Bramble Isles

Click or tap to play

An original platformer built on the snesrev/smw decomp: the code is the community reimplementation; every level, map, picture, tune, sound and line of text is new. No original game files are needed or included.

Arrows move · Z jump (B) · X spin jump (A) · A/S run, grab (Y/X) · C/V L/R · Enter Start · Right Shift Select. Controllers and touch are supported.
